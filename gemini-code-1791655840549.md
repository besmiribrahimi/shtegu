## Slide 1: The Hook (The Unfair Asymmetry)

* **Headline:** We Don't Build Parking Lots. We Monetize Trapped Asphalt.
* **The Mascot Visual:** High-contrast Shtegu winking cat with the glowing yellow "P" medallion[cite: 5].
* **The Reality:** 
  * Every business day in Prishtina, thousands of drivers circle Pejton, Qendër, and Qafa for 20 to 40 minutes burning fuel.
  * At the exact same moment, private driveways, residential courtyards, and clinic spots sit completely empty behind padlocks or rusty manual chains.
* **The Solution:** An autonomous micro-mobility network turning private stalls into automated, revenue-generating parking spots with zero civil work and zero street excavation.

* Leased to local dentists, pharmacies, and specialty shops for **€15 – €25 / month**.
